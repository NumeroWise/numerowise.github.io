What Is a Soul Urge Number?

Introduction

The Soul Urge Number is one of the most personal numbers in numerology. It reveals your inner desires, emotional needs, dreams, motivations, and the things that truly fulfill you deep inside.

While your Destiny Number describes your life direction and your Life Path Number describes your journey, your Soul Urge Number reflects what your heart genuinely wants.

How Is It Calculated?

The Soul Urge Number is calculated using only the vowels in your full birth name.

The vowels A, E, I, O, and U are assigned numerological values and added together. The total is then reduced to a single digit from 1 to 9.

What Does It Reveal?

Your Soul Urge Number can reveal:

  • Your deepest desires
  • Your emotional motivations
  • Your hidden dreams
  • Your inner strengths
  • Your spiritual needs
  • Your relationship expectations

Why Is It Important?

Many people focus only on external success, but true happiness comes from understanding what your soul genuinely seeks. Knowing your Soul Urge Number can help you make better life choices and align with your authentic self.
