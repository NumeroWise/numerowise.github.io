Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Life Path Number?

A Life Path Number is one of the most important numbers in numerology. It represents your life purpose, natural abilities and challenges.

How is the Life Path Number calculated?

It is calculated using your complete date of birth by reducing the numbers to a single digit.

Can my Life Path Number change?

No. Your Life Path Number remains the same throughout your life because it is based on your birth date.
