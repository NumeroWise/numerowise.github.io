Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Life Path Number?

Your Life Path Number represents your life purpose, strengths, talents and challenges based on your date of birth.

How is the Life Path Number calculated?

It is calculated by reducing the digits of your complete date of birth into a single number using standard numerology principles.

Can my Life Path Number change?

No. Your Life Path Number remains the same throughout your life because it is based on your birth date.

Why is the Life Path Number important?

It helps you understand your natural abilities, opportunities, challenges and overall life direction.
