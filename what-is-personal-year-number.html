What Is a Personal Year Number?

A Personal Year Number is one of the most useful concepts in numerology. It reveals the energy, opportunities, lessons, and challenges that may influence your year.

Unlike a Life Path Number, which stays the same throughout life, a Personal Year Number changes every year.

How Is a Personal Year Number Calculated?

A Personal Year Number is calculated using:

  • Your birth month
  • Your birth day
  • The current year
