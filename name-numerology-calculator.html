Frequently Asked Questions

What is Name Numerology?

Name Numerology analyzes the letters in your name to reveal your personality traits, strengths, talents, challenges and the unique energy your name carries.

How is the Name Number calculated?

Each letter in your name is assigned a numerical value using the Pythagorean numerology system. These values are added together and reduced to obtain your Name Number.

Can changing my name affect numerology?

Yes. In numerology, a name change may alter the vibration associated with your name, which can influence how you express yourself and how others perceive you.

Why is Name Numerology important?

It helps you understand the influence of your name on your personality, career, relationships and overall life direction.
