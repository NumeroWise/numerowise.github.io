What Is Business Name Numerology?

Introduction

Business Name Numerology is the study of how the numerical vibration of a business name may influence branding, customer perception, business identity, opportunities and long-term growth. Many entrepreneurs use numerology as an additional tool when selecting or evaluating a business name.

According to numerology, every letter carries a specific numerical value. When the letters of a business name are converted into numbers and added together, they produce a Business Name Number that represents the overall energy associated with that name.

How Does Business Name Numerology Work?

Each letter of the business name is assigned a numerical value based on a numerology system. These values are added together and reduced to a single digit or recognized Master Number. The resulting number is interpreted to understand the symbolic characteristics and vibration of the business name.

While business success ultimately depends on strategy, product quality, customer service, marketing and consistent effort, numerology can provide another helpful factor to consider when choosing a business name.
