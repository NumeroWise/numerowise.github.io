Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Personality Number?

Your Personality Number reveals how other people perceive you. It reflects your outward behavior, first impressions and the qualities you naturally express to the world.

How is the Personality Number calculated?

It is calculated using only the consonants in your full birth name based on the Pythagorean numerology system.

Can my Personality Number change?

No. Your Personality Number remains the same because it is derived from your birth name.

Why is the Personality Number important?

It helps you understand your public image, communication style, social strengths and how others typically respond to your personality.
