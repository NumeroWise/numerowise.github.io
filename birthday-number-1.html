You have the ability to take initiative, inspire others, and pursue goals with determination. Your confidence and originality often help you stand out and achieve success through your own efforts.

Challenges

Leadership roles, entrepreneurship, management, sales, marketing, politics, innovation, and creative professions are often well suited to your natural talents and drive.
