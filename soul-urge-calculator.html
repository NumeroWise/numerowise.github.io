Your Soul Urge Number, also known as the Heart's Desire Number, reveals your deepest inner desires, motivations, emotional needs and what truly brings you happiness.

It is calculated using only the vowels in your full birth name according to the Pythagorean numerology system.

No. Your Soul Urge Number remains constant because it is derived from your birth name.
