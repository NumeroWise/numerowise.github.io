What Is Name Numerology?

Introduction

Name Numerology is the study of how the letters in your name influence your personality, communication style, talents, opportunities, relationships, career and overall life journey. According to numerology, every letter carries a unique vibration that contributes to your personal energy.

While your date of birth represents your life's blueprint, your name reflects how that energy is expressed in the world. Together they create a more complete numerology profile.

How Does Name Numerology Work?

Each letter of the alphabet is assigned a numerical value. By converting the letters of your full name into numbers and adding them together, important numerology values are calculated to reveal different aspects of your personality and life.

Why Is Your Name Important?

Your name is spoken, written and remembered thousands of times throughout your life. Many numerologists believe that this constant repetition strengthens the energetic vibration associated with your name.

For this reason, Name Numerology is often used alongside Life Path Number analysis to gain a broader understanding of an individual's numerology profile.
