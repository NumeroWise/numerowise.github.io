Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Career Number?

A Career Number helps identify professions, industries and work environments that naturally align with your numerology profile, talents and long-term goals.

How is the Career Number calculated?

It is calculated using your core numerology numbers, such as your Life Path Number and other key numerological influences, to suggest suitable career directions.

Can my Career Number change?

No. Your core career potential remains the same because it is based on your birth information, although your opportunities may evolve with different life cycles.

How can a Career Number help me?

It can help you choose a suitable profession, understand your work strengths, improve career planning and make more confident education or business decisions.
