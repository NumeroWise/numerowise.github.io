  • Stable income mindset
  • Hard-working nature
  • Long-term planning ability

Financial Blockages

  • Fear of financial risk
  • Resistance to change
  • Over-cautious decisions
  • Slow wealth growth mindset

Improvement Strategy

Financial growth improves when Number 4 learns flexibility, accepts calculated risks, and explores new income opportunities.

Conclusion

Financial Blockage Number 4 teaches that discipline must be balanced with adaptability for financial success.
